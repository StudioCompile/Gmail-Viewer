Mail Viewer

A simple, read-only way to browse your Gmail inbox in the browser — no ads, no tracking, nothing stored on any server.

What this app does

Mail Viewer connects to your Gmail account with your permission and shows a clean, simple list of your messages, with the ability to open and read any message in full.

  • Sign in with your Google account
  • Browse your inbox in a simple, distraction-free list
  • Click any message to read the full email
  • Sign in with more than one Google account if you'd like

What it can't do

Mail Viewer only requests read-only access to Gmail. It cannot send, delete, or modify your email in any way, and it cannot access anything else in your Google account.

Your data

Mail Viewer has no backend server. Everything happens directly between your browser and Google — nothing you see is copied, logged, or stored anywhere outside your own device.
