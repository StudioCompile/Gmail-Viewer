# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 16, 2026

Gmail Viewer ("the App") is a personal, open-source project available at
https://github.com/StudioCompile/Gmail-Viewer. This policy explains how the App
handles data when you use it.

## What the App Does

The App lets a signed-in Google user view their own Gmail inbox (subject,
sender, and snippet of recent messages) directly in their browser.

## Data We Access

When you connect your Google account, the App requests **read-only** access
to your Gmail (`gmail.readonly` scope). This means the App can view your
emails but cannot send, delete, modify, or permanently alter anything in
your mailbox.

## Data We Collect and Store

**We do not collect, store, log, or transmit your email data to any server.**

The App is a static webpage with no backend. All communication happens
directly between your browser and Google's servers using Google's own
sign-in system (Google Identity Services) and the Gmail API. Your access
token and email data exist only in your browser's memory for the current
session and are not saved, persisted, or sent anywhere else.

We do not use cookies, analytics, or third-party trackers.

## Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or disclose any data to third parties, because we
never receive or store any data in the first place.

## Data Retention

Since no data is stored by the App, there is nothing for us to retain or
delete. Your Google access token expires automatically per Google's
standard token lifetime, and you can revoke the App's access at any time
(see below).

## Revoking Access

You can revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time via:
https://myaccount.google.com/permissions

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone
under the age required to hold a Google account in their jurisdiction.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the App changes. Updates will be posted to
this file in the GitHub repository.
